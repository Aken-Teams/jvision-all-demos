US 11,842,391 B2 Claim 1 4 個限制
1. An actuator control system comprising: a first sensor configured to generate a first feedback signal indicative of an output torque; a second sensor arranged along a transmission path and configured to generate a second feedback signal; a controller configured to receive the first and second feedback signals and calculate a compensated torque value; and a drive circuit configured to adjust an actuator output based on the compensated torque value within a predetermined sampling interval.
