主要欄位與更新方式

customer_key 為去識別化客戶鍵；lifetime_value 以近 24 個月淨營收及預估續約價值計算；value_segment 每小時依最新交易重新分類。

AVG(lifetime_value) AS avg_ltv
FROM analytics.customer_value_hourly
WHERE snapshot_at = (SELECT MAX(snapshot_at)
FROM analytics.customer_value_hourly)
GROUP BY value_segment;
